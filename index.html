/ 03 — Before your first game

FAQ

The practical things people ask before they meet GNDBros.

Are there membership dues?

Yes. Dues are KRW 3,000 every six months.

Do I need my own equipment?

No. Request a rental before the game and the team will help find suitable equipment.

Is there a team uniform?

No. Wear what you like as long as it follows the team rules.

Can I use HPA or a binary trigger?

No. GNDBros restricts both HPA systems and binary triggers. Ask the team before using unfamiliar equipment.

Do I have to play every game?

No. You can come along, spend time with the team, and observe the game atmosphere without playing.

Is there an age requirement?

Members must be 18 or older. Minors may only participate with a guardian present.

Does the team own a field?

Not yet. The team visits different fields depending on each game.

How do we get to the field?

Public transport is the default, but members often share rides with teammates who drive.

Is protective gear required?

Goggles are mandatory. A face mask is optional but strongly recommended to protect your teeth and face.

Are the members normal?

Unfortunately, no. Many claim otherwise. Do not believe them.

Start something
different.

From experienced players to complete beginners, there is room to start at your own pace. Follow GNDBros and ask anything before your first game.
