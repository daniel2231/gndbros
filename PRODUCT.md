# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: static HTML/CSS/JS so the sample can be opened or hosted immediately without a build step

## Users

Primary audience is young Korean airsoft players and curious visitors discovering the GNDBros crew online. They are looking for a team with a strong point of view, a sense of belonging, and a reason to follow or meet the group.

## Product Purpose

The landing page introduces GNDBros as a Korean airsoft team made up mainly of people in their 20s. Success means a first-time visitor understands the team’s personality within seconds and has a clear path to meet the crew or follow along.

## Positioning

GNDBros is presented as a personality-led crew and shared ritual, not a formal club or generic military hobby group.

## Operating Context

The page is a public-facing landing page that should work as a lightweight sample and be easy to adapt with real team photos, social links, and event details later.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Dark mode is the default.
- Palette is primarily black, white, and signal red.
- Animation is encouraged but must remain usable with reduced-motion preferences.
- The connected Notion page is the source of truth for public team facts and FAQ content.
- No individual member roster or event schedule is published on this landing page.

## Brand Commitments

Name: GNDBros. Voice: young, unique, expressive, and a little irreverent. User references: Love & Money and Anduril.

## Evidence on Hand

- User-provided Notion landing page: https://daniel2231.notion.site/c01236554ddf467c808037b120c40ba0?source=copy_link
- Verified from the connected Notion workspace: 36 members; activities beyond airsoft; mixed professional backgrounds; beginner-friendly participation; team FAQ; Instagram `@gndbros_airsoft`.
- User description: Korean airsoft team, mainly 20s, high personality.
- Generated hero image: assets/gndbros-field.png

## Product Principles

- Make the crew feel human before making them feel tactical.
- Use a strong point of view instead of generic airsoft language.
- Invite people into the world with a clear next step.
- Keep the sample honest about what is known and what still needs real team content.
